Apple Inc. was founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne in Los Altos, California. The company was originally named Apple Computer, Inc. and dropped "Computer" from its name in 2007 to reflect its expansion beyond personal computers. Apple is headquartered at Apple Park in Cupertino, California, a campus that opened to employees in 2017.

Tim Cook has served as Chief Executive Officer of Apple since August 2011, succeeding co-founder Steve Jobs. Luca Maestri served as Apple's Chief Financial Officer until 2024, when Kevan Parekh was named to succeed him in the role.

For its fiscal year 2024, which ended September 28, 2024, Apple reported total net sales of $391.035 billion, an increase of about 2 percent compared to fiscal year 2023. Net income for fiscal year 2024 was $93.736 billion.

The main contributors to fiscal 2024 revenue were iPhone, which generated $201.183 billion, Services, which generated $96.169 billion, Wearables, Home and Accessories, which generated $37.005 billion, Mac, which generated $29.984 billion, and iPad, which generated $26.694 billion.

Services revenue, which includes the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, AppleCare, and Apple Pay, grew approximately 13 percent year over year and was the fastest-growing of Apple's reportable segments in fiscal 2024.

Greater China revenue declined slightly in fiscal 2024 amid continued smartphone competition from domestic manufacturers such as Huawei and Xiaomi. The Americas remained Apple's largest reportable geographic segment by revenue in fiscal 2024.

Apple's gross margin for fiscal 2024 was approximately 46.2 percent, up from 44.1 percent in fiscal 2023, driven in part by the growth of higher-margin Services revenue. Research and development expense for fiscal 2024 was $31.37 billion, reflecting continued investment in silicon, software, and new product categories.

Apple repurchased approximately $95 billion of its own common stock during fiscal 2024 as part of its ongoing capital return program. The company also paid dividends of roughly $15.2 billion to shareholders in fiscal 2024. Apple's board of directors authorized an additional $110 billion share repurchase program in May 2024.

The iPhone 15 lineup, released in September 2023, was the primary iPhone product sold throughout most of fiscal 2024, with the iPhone 16 lineup launching just before the fiscal year closed. Apple Vision Pro, a mixed-reality headset, launched in the United States in February 2024 and expanded to additional countries later in the year, though it remains a small contributor to overall revenue.

Apple Intelligence, a suite of generative AI features integrated across iOS, iPadOS, and macOS, was announced at WWDC in June 2024 and began rolling out later that year.

Apple continues to face antitrust scrutiny in multiple jurisdictions, including a lawsuit filed by the U.S. Department of Justice in March 2024 alleging that Apple illegally maintains a monopoly over the smartphone market. The European Union's Digital Markets Act required Apple to make several changes to the App Store and iOS in the EU during 2024, including allowing alternative app marketplaces.

Apple's main competitors in smartphones include Samsung and various Android device makers, while in personal computing it competes with companies such as Dell, HP, and Lenovo.

Apple has committed to making its entire supply chain carbon neutral by 2030 and reported that it was carbon neutral for its corporate operations as of 2020. The company employed approximately 164,000 full-time equivalent employees as of September 2024.

Apple's Services segment includes the App Store, which hosts millions of third-party applications and generates revenue primarily through commissions on paid app and in-app purchase transactions. Warranty costs and litigation reserves are recorded as part of Apple's operating expenses and can vary from quarter to quarter.

Apple's effective tax rate for fiscal 2024 was affected by a one-time charge related to a European Commission state aid decision concerning Ireland. Foreign currency fluctuations had a modest unfavorable impact on Apple's reported revenue growth in fiscal 2024.

Apple typically releases its fiscal fourth-quarter and full-year results in late October or early November, with the fiscal 2024 results announced on October 31, 2024.
